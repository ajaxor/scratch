# Hollowmere artwork

Three original assets were requested through the built-in image-generation tool. They are composed as game artwork, not screenshots of an interface. WebP is used for delivery.

## Title background

A forgotten subterranean monastery in painterly pixel art: teal moonlit flooded stone, a monumental arch, moss and hanging roots, ancient columns, and a small hooded traveler carrying a golden lantern. Deep blue-black shadows, restrained teal and amber palette, no words or interface. Landscape composition with atmospheric space on the left for the game title.

## Explorer and environment atlas

A precise 4 × 4 grid of top-down / three-quarter RPG sprites on a transparent background. Row 1: hooded traveler facing down, up, left, right. Row 2: stone floor, mossy wall, dark turquoise water, lit stone doorway. Row 3: lantern, short sword, grappling hook, blue prism. Row 4: vines, crystal pedestal, treasure chest, ghostly keeper. Consistent detailed pixel art and teal/gold lighting.

## Puzzle prop atlas

A precise 4 × 4 grid of top-down / three-quarter sprites. Row 1: bronze hand bell, blue hourglass, stone weight, brass pressure plate. Row 2: mirrors in both orientations, pressure wheel, clock. Row 3: unlit and lit braziers, inscription tablet, stair dais. Row 4: mossy flagstone, purple slate, terracotta stone, midnight blue flagstone. Isolated transparent objects, full-cell floor textures, no text or interface.

## Opening-chapter furnishings

`dist/assets/furnishings.webp` was generated once with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded to WebP with its alpha preserved. It is a 1254 × 1254 image consumed as sixteen equally sized cells; cell coordinates intentionally support fractional source dimensions.

Prompt: “Production sprite atlas for the Hollowmere browser RPG. One square RGBA image with a genuinely transparent background; sixteen sprites in an equally spaced 4 × 4 grid. Detailed painterly pixel art, top-down three-quarter RPG view, abandoned monastery furnishings, weathered wood, tarnished bronze, mossy teal shadows, gentle warm highlights. Row 1: closed glass-front wooden cabinet; same cabinet open; rope pulley with weight raised; rope pulley with weight lowered. Row 2: closed shutters; open shutters; ceramic planter with leafy plant; wooden laundry rack with pale linen. Row 3: low bench; open old ledger without readable writing; wooden bowl with three copper staples; empty guest chair. Row 4: rolled sail; bronze pipe elbow; small handcart with a metal ring; faded cloth curtain. Distinct silhouettes, transparent padding, no grid, labels, numbers, scene, or watermark.”
